\chapter{Conclusion}

\section{Summary of objectives}
The thesis investigated whether symbolic chain-of-thought distillation (SCoTD) can improve a 3B-class small language model’s math reasoning accuracy on GSM8K under strict greedy evaluation, and compared rationale+answer supervision to answer-only (label-only) fine-tuning within a constrained single-GPU environment.

\section{Answers to research questions}
\textbf{RQ1}: SCoTD improved the student from 70.51\% (base with CoT prompting) to 78.54\% (+8.03 points). Thus, supervised rationale transfer is effective for multi-step arithmetic in a 3B model under resource constraints.

\textbf{RQ2}: Label-only fine-tuning improved from 10.99\% to 15.01\% (+4.02 points) but remained far below SCoTD. Intermediate supervision is substantially more informative than final-answer-only supervision for this task and evaluation method.

\section{Key conclusions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Distilling structured teacher reasoning traces yields significant accuracy gains without increased inference-time cost.
    \item Rationale diversity (30 generations/question) combined with correctness filtering and deduplication produces a high-signal training set.
    \item Parameter-efficient fine-tuning provides adequate adaptation capacity for reasoning improvements in small models under tight resource constraints.
    \item Strict formatting multiplies penalties for minor output deviations, especially for label-only training; evaluation design noticeably affects reported performance.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations recap}
Single dataset and model, absence of self-consistency decoding, and lack of formatting error analysis limit generalizability and depth of insights. Future work should address these gaps.

\section{Next steps}
Extend to additional reasoning benchmarks, model sizes and architectures, analyze formatting error influence on evaluation, experiment with self-consistency, and test distillation loss.

\section{Closing}
Symbolic chain-of-thought distillation offers a cost-efficient method to elevate small model reasoning without reliance on large teacher inference at deployment time. Under constrained hardware, combining high-quality rationale curation with parameter-efficient fine-tuning yields meaningful accuracy gains and provides a reproducible path toward more capable small-scale reasoning systems.
